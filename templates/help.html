Help & FAQ

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Register" link in the navigation bar and fill out the registration form with your details.

How do I add items to my cart?

Browse our products and click the "Add to Cart" button on any product you like. The cart icon will show the number of items.

How do I use the wishlist?

Click the heart icon next to any product to add it to your wishlist. You can view all your saved items on the wishlist page.

How does the search work?

Use the search bar in the navigation to search for products by name or description. Results will appear instantly.

What is the dynamic theming?

Our site features a unique color-changing theme that cycles through beautiful color palettes every 10 seconds for a fresh experience.

How do I contact customer support?

Visit our Contact page to send us a message, or email us at info@velorastore.com. We're here to help!

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and other secure payment methods. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping is available for faster delivery. Check your order confirmation for exact dates.

Still need help?

Can't find what you're looking for? Our customer support team is here to help.
